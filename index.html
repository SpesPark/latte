You shouldn't have to remember a toggle.

Every other "stay awake" utility makes you flip a switch before each meeting and remember to flip it back after. Forget once and your Mac sleeps mid-call, or your battery dies overnight.

Latte reads your context — Calendar events, running apps, Wi-Fi network, Focus mode — and decides for you. The cup fills when your day needs it. Empties when it doesn't.

How it works

📅 Calendar trigger

Stays awake during your scheduled meetings. Lead/trail times configurable. Works with any calendar EventKit can read.

🎥 App trigger

Active when Zoom, Teams, Webex, Discord, Slack, or any app you choose is running. Real friendly names + icons in the picker.

📡 Wi-Fi trigger

Awake on your work network. Sleeps on your home Wi-Fi. Or invert it — your call.

🌙 Focus trigger

Pairs with macOS Focus modes. Awake when "Work" is on, sleeps when "Personal" takes over.

What you don't have to think about

No telemetry. No analytics. No network calls. All settings live in your local UserDefaults. Your calendar is read-only and never leaves your Mac.
